Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

Fast Eleven has no accounts. Your careers, squads and settings are stored only on your device, and the game plays offline. A few parts of the app use services from other companies; they are described below.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The free version shows a short full-screen ad between matches, after every third match you play, never during a match. Ads are served by Google AdMob, which collects data from your device to show and measure ads: an advertising identifier (on iOS only if you allow tracking), your approximate location from your IP address, which ads you saw and interacted with, and diagnostic and performance data. Ads are limited to content suitable for all ages.

Where the law requires it (for example in the EU, the UK and Brazil), the app asks for your consent before any ad, and on iOS it shows Apple's tracking prompt. If you say no, you still see ads, but they are not personalised. You can change your choices any time in Settings › Store › "Ad Privacy Choices" (shown where consent is required), in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking, or in your Android device's ad settings. Google explains how it uses this data at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

The one-time "Remove Ads" purchase turns ads off for good, and the AdMob SDK is then not started at all.

Analytics and crash reports (Google Firebase)

Firebase Analytics records which screens you open and game events (such as matches played), together with an app-instance identifier, your device model, OS version and approximate location from your IP address. Firebase Crashlytics sends a report when the app crashes, with technical details such as device model, OS version and the error. We use this to fix bugs and understand which features are used.

Game data and feedback

When the app starts with an internet connection, it downloads the latest club and player data from our server. That request contains no personal data. If you send feedback from the app, the text you write is sent to our server together with your platform, OS version and app version. Please don't include personal information in it.

Purchases

"Remove Ads" is an in-app purchase processed by Apple or Google. We never see your payment details or account; the game only asks the store whether the purchase was made, so it can restore it.

Children

The game has no chat and no user-generated content. Ads are limited to general-audience content.

Changes and contact

If a future version changes what is collected, this page will be updated with a new date before that version is released. For privacy questions, see the support page.
